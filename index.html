What we treat

Pain has a cause. We look for it.

Most people arrive after months of managing around a problem. The first session is about finding what's actually driving it — not just treating where it hurts.

Back & neck pain

Desk-related strain, disc irritation, and the stiffness that arrives after a long drive or a bad night.

Sports injuries

Sprains, tendon problems, and return-to-sport planning that doesn't send you straight back into the same injury.

Post-surgery recovery

Structured rehabilitation after knee, hip, or shoulder surgery, working alongside your surgeon's protocol.

Shoulder & knee

Frozen shoulder, rotator cuff pain, patellar tracking problems, and joints that give way without warning.

Workplace strain

Repetitive strain, wrist and forearm pain, and the postural load that builds up over years at a desk or on a site.

Persistent pain

Pain that has outlasted the injury that caused it, where the goal is restoring confidence in movement.

How it works

Three stages, in this order.

No open-ended courses of treatment. You'll know after the first session what the plan is and roughly how long it should take.

STAGE 01

Assessment

Forty-five minutes. We take a history, measure range and strength, and test the movements that reproduce your pain. You leave with an explanation, not a leaflet.

STAGE 02

Treatment plan

Hands-on work in the clinic, plus a short home programme built around what you'll realistically do. Four exercises you complete beats twelve you abandon.

STAGE 03

Progress reviews

We re-measure at each review and adjust. When your numbers hold steady without treatment, we discharge you rather than book you in again.
